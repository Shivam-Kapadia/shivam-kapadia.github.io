I'm a Computer Engineering student at UIUC who outworks the problem. I take on things I haven't done before, go all-in, and don't stop until they work.

A people-operations workspace that puts the employee directory, onboarding automation and kanban task boards in one place, with Slack sync. PalmSlash: Webcam Fruit Ninja

A Fruit Ninja clone with no mouse and no keyboard, just your hands in the air. MediaPipe tracks both hands and index fingertips become blades. A One-Euro filter running at 60fps smooths and predicts between detection samples throttled to about 22Hz, so tracking stays fluid even though inference runs far below the frame rate. A slice is tested as the segment between a fingertip's last two positions against each fruit's hitbox, gated by a minimum speed so a resting finger doesn't shred the screen. Ships as a single ~1,100-line index.html with no build step.
